## Changed in v2026.31

* Announcements: overlapping sounds are no longer completely suppressed. The exact same sound cannot play simultaneously, but if you see e.g. a rare and a mount-rare and a piece of junk loot all at the same time they'll all play
* Browser:
    * The right-click menu for top-level categories now shows the option to ignore a whole source, not just individual mobs, and marks a source's heading when it's ignored
    * "Only ignored"/"Hide ignored" filters now account for whole sources being ignored, not just individually-ignored mobs
    * A shortcut to the options is now in both the icon-menu and the filter menu
    * Renamed "Dim the rest of the zone" to "Show other rares in the zone"
* Mobs: slightly backtracking, a "Sources" tab now lists every data source SilverDragon knows about and lets you toggle each one off, without needing to dig into a submenu of the browser filter options
* Loot window: rearranged and shrunk, with loot you can't get on your current character split out from the rest
* Loot window tooltips no longer show up underneath AppearanceTooltip's overlays
* Shadowlands:
    * Bastion: the Ascended Council shows a single icon instead of one per member; added the Lost Soul for Night Fae
    * The Maw: fixed the map positions of Zeris and Dolos
