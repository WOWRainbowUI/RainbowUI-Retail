- Leave health-only Bobbing Apple (and Forever's Cooked Crab Claw) out of the AutoDrink macro (#158)  
    Bobbing Apple casts Food 24869 on Classic Era, TBC, Wrath and Forever, which restores health  
    only (Cata and Mists use 24707, which restores mana too). Forever's Cooked Crab Claw casts  
    Nutritious Food (Food 433 plus an Intellect Well Fed), also without mana. Both stay in the  
    AutoFood lists.
